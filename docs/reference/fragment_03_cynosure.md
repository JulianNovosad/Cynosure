# Fragment 3: Cynosure — Militech's Rogue AI Containment

**Cynosure** — Militech's answer to Arasaka's Soulkiller. A secret project to capture and weaponize rogue AIs from the Net.

During the pre-DataKrash era, Militech and Arasaka raced for digital dominance. Soulkiller pushed Militech to develop Cynosure beneath Night City at Site C, using neural adaptive tech to trap AIs lurking below the sanitized Net and turn them into weapons. The 2022 DataKrash wrecked much of the Net's infrastructure and scattered the very AIs Militech hoped to capture.

Decades later, Militech attempted reactivation. In 2068, researchers including Dr. Evan McRay and the Cassel twins accessed the old facility, contacted AIs beyond the Blackwall, and built prototypes like the Canto cyberdeck and Erebus SMG. But geopolitical fallout from the Unification War, plus treaties banning Blackwall probing, forced them to abandon the project.

In 2077, the rogue netrunner Song So Mi fell into cyberpsychosis and fled into the deactivated Cynosure facility. Rogue AIs possessed her, forcing her to activate a Militech Cerberus unit to kill the mercenary V. V reached the core, shut it down, and ended the threat. The facility's fate remains uncertain.

**Database Entry:** Officially, almost nothing is known. Unofficially, even less. Cynosure was likely an experimental weapon/tool to capture rogue AIs for Militech's military and intelligence edge—possibly a Soulkiller competitor. Some say accidents killed it; others claim the AIs broke free, or that Arasaka/NetWatch exposure forced a cover-up.

---

*Note: This project is named Cynosure after this facility. It is designed to capture rogue AI, but not only that. Making, designing, using, tweaking, modifying, etc. of rogue AI is also part of this project.*
