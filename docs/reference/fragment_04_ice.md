# Fragment 4: ICE — Intrusion Countermeasures Electronics

ICE (Intrusion Countermeasures Electronics) is the most advanced Net security—software protecting individuals and infrastructures from breach. Every corporation fields netrunner divisions to harden their data fortresses; every netrunner hunts for backdoors. Cut through thick ICE without skill and your synapses get fried.

**Notable ICEs:**
- **Blackwall** — NetWatch ICE guarding against rogue AIs.
- **Black ICE** — Functions within NET Architectures.
- **Self-ICE** — Frontal cortex implant.

*Use this mostly as directional inspiration, not actionable descriptions.*
