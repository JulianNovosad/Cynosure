> The Cyberpunk "Net" diverged from ours around 1990. Instead of evolving through a hypertext-based environment, the early text-based "AOL style" Net jumped straight into a shared 3D space by 2013. Rather than dispersed independent sites with URLs, the 2020 Net is like a giant AOL or Compuserve with "REALLY GREAT GRAPHICS." Netrunners travel to enormous walled-garden Net Zones (Atlantis, Pacifica, etc.). In 2013, accessing these zones required downloading "interface" preloads—like MMO patches. By 2020, the Ihara/Grubb equations created a shared universal rendering standard, eliminating the need for preloaded interfaces thanks to superior bandwidth.
